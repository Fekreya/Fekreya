Common questions

FAQ. Short answers, no fluff.

Anything missing? Reply to your booking confirmation and ask.

Where are your assistants based?

Across multiple time zones. We match each client with an assistant whose working hours overlap yours. The location matters less than the fit. All assistants are vetted personally, English-fluent, and tech-savvy.

What if I don't like my assistant?

Tell us within the first 30 days and we'll re-match you within 7 days, no charge. After 30 days, we'll still re-match. We just need a heads-up so we can find the right fit. We track satisfaction monthly so problems surface early.

How is this different from a generic VA agency?

Three things. (1) You get a dedicated assistant, not a pool. (2) They're trained on AI workflows, not just inbox triage. (3) You get a branded client hub showing live tasks, reports, and recordings, so you always know what's happening without asking.

Do I need to manage my assistant?

No. Each engagement comes with an account manager (us) who handles performance, coverage, escalations. You give feedback once a month. We run the operation. You get the output.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes. Every assistant signs a client NDA before being matched. Access to your tools is granted via a password manager (we never see raw credentials). Deletion-on-offboarding is contractually guaranteed.

What about weekends and time off?

Standard plans cover Monday to Friday in your time zone. Weekend coverage is included with Full-Time, optional add-on with Part-Time. When your primary assistant takes time off, your cross-trained backup steps in seamlessly.

Can I scale up or down later?

Yes. Move between Project, Part-Time, and Full-Time month-to-month with 14 days' notice. No annual lock-in, no cancellation fee.

How do you ensure quality?

Three layers. (1) Every assistant is personally vetted before being added to the roster. (2) Weekly Friday reports keep work visible. (3) Monthly client check-ins surface anything before it becomes a problem. We catch issues in week one, not month three.

Ready for your week back?

15 minutes on a call. We learn your business. You learn whether this fits. No deck, no pressure. If it's a yes, you have a working assistant in 10 days.
