Common questions

FAQ.

Quick answers.

Where are your executive assistants based?

In your time zone. Vetted, trained, fluent in your tools. Location matters less than fit.

What if the fit isn't right?

Tell us early. We'd rather adjust than lose you. Most matches settle in within the first two weeks.

How is this different from hiring a virtual assistant?

We don't run a marketplace. We don't send a pool. You get one person, picked for you, trained on how your business actually runs. We hire them, train them, and stay involved.

Do I have to manage them?

No. We do. You give feedback when something matters. We run the operation behind the scenes.

What about confidentiality?

Every executive assistant signs a client confidentiality agreement. Credentials live in a password manager. Access is removed the moment a relationship ends.

Weekends and time off?

You pick the five days that work for you. A quiet backup steps in when your EA is away.

Can I move between packages?

Yes, any time. Built around you.

How do you handle quality?

Vetted on entry. Weekly reports. Monthly check-ins. We catch issues in week one, not month three.
