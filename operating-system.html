The Idea

Fekreya is an Egypt-based agency placing trained executive assistants with global SMB owners.

You handle relationship + matching + monitoring. Your assistants handle execution. Clients get premium-quality support at half the US cost. The operating system below is everything you need to run this from solo to your first 20 clients.

Why this works in 2026

SMB owners worldwide are time-broke and AI-curious. They want help but can't justify hiring full-time. The post-COVID norm of remote work means a sharp, English-fluent assistant in Cairo is functionally indistinguishable from one in Austin — at 40–60% the cost. Egypt has a deep pool of bilingual graduates and a favorable USD/EGP rate. Fekreya is the bridge.

The principle

You are not the chief assistant — you are the conductor. The moment you start doing VA work for a client because "it's just easier this once," you've stopped being able to scale. Every task that comes to you should bounce to a VA, even if it takes longer the first time. That's the whole game.

Workflow

Cold outreach — from dataset to first call

Hyperpersonalized, not bulk. The system below produces ~30 deeply-personalized sends per week and books 3–5 calls. That's enough to grow steadily. You can scale up by hiring a junior outreach VA later.

1

Curate your batch (10 prospects at a time)

From your dataset, pick 10 prospects who fit the ICP (SMB owner, English-speaking, business obviously time-strapped — realtors with active listings, coaches with social presence, etc.). Drop them in the HubSpot pipeline as "New."

Tools: HubSpot · Apollo (verify emails)
2

Generate personalized PDF for each

Hand each prospect to Claude: name, business, website. Claude researches them, writes the "I noticed" hook, customizes 2–3 sections of the VA pitch template, generates a branded PDF named VA-Proposal-[BusinessName].pdf.

Tools: Claude · the VA pitch template
3

Send 4-line email with PDF attached

Subject: "Made this for [Business Name]". Body: 4 lines max — personal observation + "made you something specific" + soft "delete if not useful." PDF attached. Sent from Instantly.ai (warmed fekreya.com domain).

Tools: Instantly.ai · Gmail (Workspace) for warmer 1-on-1s
4

One follow-up after 5 days · then move on

Single follow-up: "Just floating this back up — no pressure." If no reply, mark "Cold" in HubSpot and stop. Re-add to a 90-day reactivation list. There are always more prospects.

Tools: HubSpot pipeline + Instantly sequence
5

Reply → Calendly link → 15-min intro call

When someone replies interested, immediately drop your Calendly link. The call is 15 minutes — confirm fit, hear their day-to-day, share 2–3 specific things a VA would handle for them. End with: "I'll send a customized proposal by tomorrow."

Tools: Calendly · Google Meet · Fathom (auto-records)
6

Customized proposal → signed agreement

Use Claude + Fathom transcript to produce the customized v2 proposal (now with their actual workflows, not generic). Send + simple monthly agreement (Stripe link or invoice). On signed, hand off to onboarding workflow.

Tools: Claude · Stripe / Wise for invoicing
Outreach math

Hyperpersonalized at this depth: expect 15–25% open rates, 5–10% reply rates, 1–3% conversion to paid client. Per 100 sends → ~2 new clients. Per month at 30 sends/week → 4–6 new clients/month at full ramp. That fills your VA roster fast.

Workflow

Client onboarding — signed to live in 10 days

The promise made in your pitch deck. This workflow delivers it. Repeat for every new client without thinking — that's why it's documented.

1

Day 0 — Welcome email + intake form

Within 1 hour of signed agreement: warm welcome email + a Google Form / Typeform intake. Captures their tools (CRM, calendar, email), top 5 time-drains, communication style preferences, weekend coverage needs.

Tools: Gmail · Typeform or Google Forms
2

Day 1–2 — Match a VA + spin up infrastructure

Pick the VA whose skills + time-zone overlap fits. Create their ClickUp folder for this client. Spin up the client's hub at fekreya.com/hub/[client-slug]. Embed ClickUp folder. Set up shared Google Drive. Invite their VA's @fekreya.com email as a delegate to the client's tools (with permission).

Tools: ClickUp · Google Drive · Cloudflare Pages
3

Day 3 — Kickoff call (45 min)

You + the VA + the client. Full intro. Walk through the hub. Get verbal consent for call recording. Define the first 5 tasks the VA will own this week. Recording auto-saved by Fathom and added to the hub for client reference.

Tools: Google Meet · Fathom
4

Day 4–7 — Shadow week

VA mirrors the client's workflow. Inbox triage drafts go to client for review before sending. You check Slack for blockers daily but stay out of the way. Adjustments captured in the SOP doc inside the client folder.

Tools: Slack · Shortwave · ClickUp
5

Day 8–10 — Full operation + first Friday report

VA running autonomously. First Friday report delivered through the hub: tasks completed, time spent, blockers, next week's plan. Client receives a Loom from the VA walking through the report.

Tools: Loom · ClickUp report widget · email
6

Day 30 — Your monthly client review

You join a 20-min call with the client. No VA. Honest check-in. Adjust scope if needed. This is also the moment to plant the seed for upgrades or referrals.

Tools: Calendly · Google Meet · Fathom

Monitoring & QA — staying informed without micromanaging

Your goal is to know what's going on with every client + VA without sitting in every meeting or reviewing every email. The system below gives you that visibility in about 30 minutes per day.

Your daily monitoring loop (30 min)
A

Morning · Slack #va-team scan (5 min)

Quick scroll. Any blocker flags from yesterday? Any clients showing dissatisfaction? Reply only where needed.

B

Midday · Fathom recordings inbox (10 min)

New client meetings auto-summarized by Fathom. Read the AI summaries (not the full transcripts). Flag any that need your follow-up.

C

Late afternoon · ClickUp dashboard sweep (10 min)

"All Clients" master view. Look for: tasks overdue more than 24 hours · clients with no activity in 3+ days · any "urgent" tag still open.

D

End of day · 1 personal touch (5 min)

Send a short Slack to one VA + one client. Different people each day. Just human. "Saw you closed Elm Street — nice." This is the glue.

Meeting recording — the right way
Important · ethical & legal

Recording a call without consent is illegal in many jurisdictions (Egypt included). Always: (1) Verbally announce at the start of every meeting that "we record our calls for quality and so my team is aligned." (2) Pause for verbal consent on the recording itself. (3) Store recordings in your private drive — never share publicly. (4) Delete recordings after 90 days unless flagged.

The Client Hub — your premium signature

Every client gets a private branded hub at fekreya.com/hub/[their-slug]. This is what makes you feel premium vs. every other VA agency. Built once as a template, customized per client in 10 minutes.

What lives inside each hub
Tasks tab
ClickUp public-share URL embedded as iframe. Shows the client their live task board, updated real-time. No "what's happening?" emails.
Calendar tab
ClickUp calendar view embedded — shows what's scheduled and when their VA is on/off.
Reports tab
Friday reports, monthly summaries, any custom deliverables (audits, plans, decks). All Looms embedded.
Documents tab
Their SOP doc, their VA's profile, recordings of their kickoff and monthly reviews, any shared assets.
"Reach me" panel
Their VA's direct Slack/email/WhatsApp. Your direct contact for escalation. Calendly to book monthly review with you.
Wins ticker
Optional · simple list of recent wins ("48 leads contacted this week", "responded to 12 reviews"). Reinforces value subliminally.

30-Day Launch Plan

From zero to first paying client in 4 weeks. This is paced for a solo founder doing this part-time. Faster is possible if you're full-time.

Week 1
Foundation — make Fekreya real
Setup phase
  • Set up Google Workspace on fekreya.com · create your mariam@fekreya.com
  • Create logo + 1-color brand (Canva or hire a 1-day designer) · simple wordmark is fine
  • Build fekreya.com landing page (Claude can do this — see "Build Next" section)
  • Set up Calendly with your booking link · embed in email signature
  • Set up HubSpot Free CRM · import your prospect dataset
  • Sign up for Instantly.ai · start domain warmup (sends 5 emails/day to test addresses for 14 days before real cold sending)
Week 2
Infrastructure — make Fekreya operational
Build phase
  • Build the Client Hub template (Claude does this) · publish at fekreya.com/hub/template
  • Build the VA Onboarding Pack — 1-page Loom + checklist + welcome doc · for every new VA
  • Set up your ClickUp workspace · create the per-client folder template
  • Sign your first VA (one of your friends/colleagues) · onboard them as the test case
  • Run a mock client engagement with a friend acting as a fake client · iterate the process before paying customers see it
Week 3
Launch outreach — first 30 sends
Selling phase
  • Domain warmup complete · start cold outreach
  • 10 personalized PDFs/day for 3 days · 30 total sends
  • Calendly fills with intake calls · take every one personally
  • Track everything in HubSpot · open rates, replies, calls booked
  • Iterate the email subject line + opening if open rates are below 20%
Week 4
First paying client + first VA placement
Live phase
  • First signed client · run them through the 10-day onboarding workflow
  • Spin up their hub · embed their ClickUp folder · invite them in
  • Their VA goes live · first Friday report sent
  • Continue outreach — 30 more sends this week
  • End of month: write a 30-day reflection · what worked, what didn't · adjust the OS doc
Honest expectation

Most founders panic in week 3 because outreach feels slow. Your first sale typically lands between days 18–35. If you've sent 30 personalized PDFs and gotten 4 calls + 1 signed by Day 30, you're on track. If you've sent 200 generic emails and gotten 0 calls, you're doing it wrong. Quality over quantity, every time.
